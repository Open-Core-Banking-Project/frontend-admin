# Phase 4 - Advanced Banking

## Objective

Add governed treasury/liquidity/FX exposure and trade-finance/correspondent operational experiences after their backend contracts, accounting treatment, risk limits, approval policies, and data sources are approved.

## Epics

| Order | Epic | Primary portal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Treasury, Liquidity, and FX Exposure | Finance/Reporting |
| 2 | Trade Finance and Correspondent Operations | Staff Operations/Risk/Finance |

## Gates

- Analytical positions and forecasts state source, as-of time, currency, rate source, and freshness.
- Frontend performs no authoritative FX, valuation, interest, exposure, limit, fee, or accounting calculation.
- Deals and instruments preserve maker-checker, multi-level approval, sanctions, document, settlement, and audit states.
- Visual analytics have accessible tabular alternatives.

## Agent instructions

These capabilities contain high-impact policy and accounting uncertainty. No epic becomes approved until contracts and decision owners are named. Backend remains read-only.
